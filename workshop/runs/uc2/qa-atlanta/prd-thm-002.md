# PRD — Administration usability

**Theme:** THM-002 — Policy editor and administrator experience
**Status:** Draft for product-owner review (not approved from this document)

## Problem

Customers keep hitting friction in this area. Grounding examples:

- FBK-0002 (CUST-002, Strategic): “Keyboard navigation in the policy editor skips conditional fields.”
- FBK-0008 (CUST-008, Public Sector): “Administrators want bulk policy editing with a safe preview.”
- FBK-0014 (CUST-014, Strategic): “The settings hierarchy is difficult for new account administrators.”

## Scope

Top-ranked backlog candidates for this theme:

- CAN-014 — Administration usability: initiative 3 (RICE 915.3)
- CAN-008 — Administration usability: initiative 2 (RICE 410.4)

## Acceptance criteria

- Behavior changes are visible in operational reporting (Pulse) where applicable.
- No catalog price changes — packaging questions route to commercial review.

## Out of scope
